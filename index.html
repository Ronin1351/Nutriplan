The easiest way to import recipes from ANY website!

Step 1: Drag this to your bookmarks bar ⬇️

🔖 Save to Nutriplan

Important: Don't click it - DRAG it to your bookmarks bar at the top of your browser!

Step 2: How to use it

  1. Visit any recipe website (AllRecipes, Food Network, etc.)
  2. Click the "🔖 Save to Nutriplan" bookmark you just added
  3. Copy the code below and save as the URL of your bookmark
